    Shorts

    What n8n will post, in order: priority, then score, then oldest. Edit the copy or put a Short on hold before it goes out.

    Style

    Which moments become Shorts, and how they should look, in your own words. Claude reads the instructions for every video and decides the hook, cold open, captions, music, zooms, sound effects and voice for each clip. Changes save on their own and apply to the next video.

    Quality & length

    Quality over quantity: a weak video can make zero Shorts. Lengths include the cold open.

    Instructions for Claude

    Without instructions, Shorts get the default look: a cold open of the payoff, a green typed-on hook, white captions with a coloured key word, a ducked beat, punch-in zooms, whooshes on cuts and a looping ending. Claude follows the subject's face and checks every clip's frames.
